# The S2 done-when, the guest: registers in the screens, finds the room in the list, joins it and plays a new
# character (the campaign bot); right after its save room it leaves the room: the automatic save when leaving finds
# nothing new (the server holds the save room's revision).
open
screen register
register $UI_B_NAME $UI_B_PASSWORD
expect-screen registered
ack
expect-screen rooms
wait-room $UI_ROOM
join $UI_ROOM
wait-session
wait-stage stagis12 1200
wait-stage stagml12 300
sleep 5
menu online
wait 10
shot guest_saved
leave
wait-left 120
sleep 2
end
