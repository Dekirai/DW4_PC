# The S2 done-when, the room owner: registers in the screens (no panel: the campaign bot plays the title), creates a
# room and plays a new character (the campaign bot from a new game, --input): its first stage change creates the online
# character, every changed stage change uploads it, the campaign's checkpoint p2 saves it in the save room (stagIS12,
# back in stagML12). It waits until b has left (the F1 Online tab refreshes the room view), plays on into stag0101
# (the campaign trains there: the character changes) and leaves the room: the automatic save when leaving uploads.
open
screen register
register $UI_A_NAME $UI_A_PASSWORD
expect-screen registered
ack
expect-screen rooms
create $UI_ROOM max=4
wait-session
wait-stage stagis12 1200
wait-stage stagml12 300
menu online
sleep 4
wait 10
shot owner_room
wait-members <=1 900
sleep 2
wait 10
shot owner_alone
menu close
wait-stage stag0101 600
sleep 40
menu online
wait 10
shot owner_field
leave
wait-left 120
sleep 2
end
