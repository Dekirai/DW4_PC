# The S2 done-when, a: registers in the screens (no panel: the campaign bot plays the title), plays in the world
# and plays a new character (the campaign bot from a new game, --input): its first stage change creates the online
# character, every changed stage change uploads it, the campaign's checkpoint p2 saves it in the save room (stagIS12,
# back in stagML12). In the world a and b are each at home (no instance holds them: wait-instance home), a plays on into stag0101
# (the campaign trains there: the character changes) and leaves the world: the automatic save when leaving uploads.
open
screen register
register $UI_A_NAME $UI_A_PASSWORD
expect-screen registered
ack
expect-screen home
play
wait-session
wait-stage stagis12 1200
wait-stage stagml12 300
menu online
sleep 4
wait 10
shot owner_room
wait-instance home
sleep 30
wait 10
shot owner_home
menu close
wait-stage stag0101 600
sleep 40
menu online
wait 10
shot owner_field
leave
wait-left 120
sleep 2
end
