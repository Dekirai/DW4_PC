# Round I1b: the player card. a hosts in stag0101 (its pad script), b joins; a opens the F1 Online tab and b's card (as A on
# its row with the controller), then works it with the controller: A on Report shows the reasons, A on Griefing sends it
# (the toast "Report sent. Thank you."); the card again, right to Remove, A shows the question, A removes b (b goes home).
open
login $UI_A_NAME $UI_A_PASSWORD
play
wait-session
wait-members 2 420
sleep 3
menu online
wait 20
card $UI_B_NAME
wait 20
shot card
key pad-a
wait 20
shot reasons
key pad-a
sleep 3
wait 10
shot reported
card $UI_B_NAME
wait 20
key pad-right
wait 10
key pad-a
wait 20
shot question
key pad-a
wait-members <=1 60
sleep 2
wait 10
shot removed
menu close
leave
wait-left 60
end
