# The world's home screen (round I1a; was the room scenario): log in, the home screen, Online > Settings (the default
# visibility Friends, camera mode 1), Play: the world session; the online title; leaving the world. After Play the
# screens stay on the playing screen (the F1 Online tab's Instance page) until B or its Play closes them, as in areas
# (fix-019's run r2b: the script had waited for them to close by themselves).
wait-title
to-menu
pad DOWN DOWN A
wait-screens
screen login
fill login.name $UI_A_NAME
wait 10
shot login
login $UI_A_NAME $UI_A_PASSWORD
expect-screen home
wait 10
shot home
screen settings
settings visibility=1 camera=1 light=1
wait 10
shot settings
play
wait-session
expect-screens open
key pad-b
sleep 1
expect-screens closed
sleep 4
wait 10
shot online_title
menu online
wait-instance home
sleep 3
wait 10
shot online_menu_home
sleep 40
leave
wait-left
sleep 2
wait 10
shot after_leave
end
