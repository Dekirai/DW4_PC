# A player with a saved character: logs in without the panel, plays, the campaign bot walks into its lobby; it leaves
# the world itself, then resets the online character its game made (the account screen's Reset online character).
open
login $UI_B_NAME $UI_B_PASSWORD
play
wait-session
sleep 30
menu online
wait 10
shot player_view
menu close
sleep 60
leave
wait-left 120
sleep 2
menu online
wait 10
shot left
# Round 56: the online character this game made (its first stage change) is reset on the account screen.
screen account
wait 10
shot account
screen reset
wait 5
shot reset
reset-character $UI_B_PASSWORD
expect-info was reset
wait 10
shot reset_done
end
