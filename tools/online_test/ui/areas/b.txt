# A guest with a saved character: logs in without the panel, joins, the campaign bot plays; a kicks it. Then it resets the
# online character its game made (the account screen's Reset online character).
open
login $UI_B_NAME $UI_B_PASSWORD
play
wait-session
sleep 30
menu online
wait 10
shot guest_view
menu close
wait-left 300
sleep 2
menu online
wait 10
shot kicked
# Round 56: the online character this game made (its first stage change) is reset on the account screen.
screen account
wait 10
shot account
screen reset
wait 5
shot reset
reset-character $UI_B_PASSWORD
expect-info was reset
wait 10
shot reset_done
end
